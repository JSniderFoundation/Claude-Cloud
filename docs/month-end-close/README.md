# Month-End Close Checklists

Two versions of the 8-day close checklist for each month from November 2026 through December 2027, for Foundation and Capital City Quartz. All files are manual workbooks with no formulas or automation.

- `starting-place/` is the lean checklist as it was actually run, rolled forward from the final September 2026 file. Three tabs: the checklist, Open Items List, Commentary. Files are named for the Day 0 date.
- `full/` is the full checklist with the longer sub-steps and evidence, plus Read Me, By Person, Close Calendar, and the two BAU tabs.

The September and October 2026 files are one folder up, in `docs/`.

## Assumptions to confirm

- **Company holidays.** Five holidays fall on or next to a close window and are treated as non-working: New Year's Day (Fri 1/1/2027), Memorial Day (Mon 5/31/2027), Independence Day observed (Mon 7/5/2027), Labor Day (Mon 9/6/2027), and New Year's Day observed (Fri 12/31/2027). Veterans Day is a working day. If the company works any of these, the affected close moves up a day.
- **Base content.** The lean files carry the wording, owners, and systems from the final September 2026 checklist. Changes made during the October 2026 close are not included.
- **Meetings.** The preliminary review, final walk-through, and review with Nick follow the September pattern (Day 6, Day 8, two business days after Day 8). Confirm each month with Phil and Nick.
